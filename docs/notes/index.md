- [Data, analytics & ML](aws/data-analytics-ml-services.md): Kinesis, MSK, Glue, EMR, Lake Formation, Athena, QuickSight, SageMaker and the AI services.
- [Security](aws/security-services.md): IAM, Identity Center, Cognito, CloudTrail, Config, GuardDuty, Inspector, Macie, KMS, WAF, Shield and Network Firewall.
- [Designing for security](aws/designs-for-security.md): the SAPTRAP design principles, the seven security areas, the shared responsibility model and securing a VPC.
- [Management & governance](aws/management-governance-services.md): CloudFormation, CDK, CloudWatch, X-Ray, Trusted Advisor, Compute Optimizer, Organizations and Control Tower.
- [Migration & transfer](aws/migration-services.md): Application Migration Service, DMS, Elastic Disaster Recovery, DataSync, Transfer Family and the Snow Family.
