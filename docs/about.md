# About Me

Hi! I'm Ganga, a hands-on DevOps, CI/CD and platform/SRE engineer based in New York. I've been in tech for over 10 years now. I started as a Java developer back in 2015, when crypto and blockchain were just breaking out. How I got from a science degree and a lab tech job in Bend, Oregon in 2011 to senior DevOps and platform engineer in 2026 is an interesting story, but it deserves a vlog of its own. The short version: I started out with different ambitions, and IT found me.

For the last 5 years I've mostly lived in the grey area where DevOps engineers roam, somewhere between development, operations and SRE. Day to day that means automation, CI/CD workflows and pipelines, containerization, Kubernetes, GitOps with Argo CD, service mesh, CNI and networking, security, certificates and all the tooling that comes with them.

In my humble opinion, DevOps isn't for everyone. It suits people who are curious about everything. Most engineers go deep in one area, whether that's development, testing, databases or networking. I've always liked moving across them, and outside of IT I'm the same way with music, politics, history, you name it. That's just how I'm wired. Over the years I've found this matters a lot in tech: two experts from different areas can talk right past each other because neither knows the other's system well enough. I'm often the person in the middle who can translate, and that's what keeps projects moving.
