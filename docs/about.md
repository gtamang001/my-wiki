# About Me

Hi! I'm Ganga, a hands-on DevOps and platform engineering leader based in New York with 10+ years in tech. These days I spend most of my time with Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, Argo CD, Terraform, Ansible, Cilium and a lot of dashboards.

My path wasn't a straight line. I started out writing Java applications, moved into automation on Linux, then got hooked on the "how does this actually run in production?" side of things. Somewhere along the way I realized I enjoy making *other* engineers' lives easier: fewer surprises, fewer manual steps, fewer "works on my machine" moments.

## What makes me tick

- **The tricky bugs.** Give me a weird networking issue and a cup of coffee and I'm happy. I like peeling back the layers (DNS, transport, TLS, HTTP, app) until the problem has nowhere left to hide.
- **Building things once, properly.** Reusable workflows and shared standards beat heroic one-off fixes every time.
- **Helping teams move faster.** I've worked with 10+ application teams and led a small team of two engineers. The best platform is the one people don't have to think about.
- **Learning in public.** This site is my notebook. If I figured something out the hard way, it goes here so nobody (including me) has to do it twice.

The full story with dates and details is on my [resume](resume.md).
